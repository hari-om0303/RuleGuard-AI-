# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Scholarships, Financial Aid & Grants (2025-2026 Edition)

## Document Information
- **Document Code**: NIT-SCHOLAR-2025-v1
- **Issuing Authority**: Student Financial Aid Board & Senate Committee on Grants
- **Scope**: Outlines eligibility criteria, application workflows, maintenance thresholds, and forfeiture rules for all merit scholarships, need-based bursaries, research assistantships, and sports endowments at Northbridge Institute of Technology.

---

## Section 17.0 Institutional Scholarship Framework

### Section 17.1 Dean's Merit Scholarship Eligibility & Renewal
The Dean's Merit Scholarship is the flagship academic honor awarded to top-ranking undergraduate students in each academic department at Northbridge Institute of Technology. To qualify for the initial award or annual renewal of the Dean's Merit Scholarship, a student must satisfy four cumulative criteria:
1. Maintain a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 9.00 or higher on a 10.00 scale at the end of each academic year.
2. Complete a minimum of eighteen (18) academic credits in each regular semester (Fall and Spring).
3. Possess zero grade backlogs, with no letter grade below 'C' earned in any course throughout enrollment.
4. Maintain a clean institutional disciplinary record with zero misconduct sanctions issued by the Proctorial Board.

The Dean's Merit Scholarship provides a 50% waiver of annual tuition fees for up to four consecutive academic years. Renewal evaluation is conducted automatically by the Student Financial Aid Board at the close of every Spring Semester.

### Section 17.2 Need-Based Financial Hardship Bursaries
Northbridge Institute of Technology provides Need-Based Financial Hardship Bursaries to assist enrolled students experiencing severe documented personal or family financial distress. Need-based bursaries cover between 25% and 75% of tuition fees depending on verified annual household income. To apply, students must submit certified tax returns, income declarations, and a letter of financial hardship to the Financial Aid Office by July 15 for Fall Semester consideration or December 15 for Spring Semester consideration.

### Section 17.3 Women in STEM Leadership Grants
To encourage gender diversity in technical disciplines, the Institute offers the Women in STEM Leadership Grant to eligible female undergraduate students pursuing degrees in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or Robotics. Recipients receive an annual stipend of $2,000 toward learning resources and laboratory equipment, alongside priority mentorship placement with industry partners. Applicants must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.50 to retain grant eligibility.

## Section 18.0 Graduate Research Assistantships & Fellowships

### Section 18.1 Master's Teaching Assistantship (TA) Appointments
Full-time Master of Technology (M.Tech) students admitted under regular merit quotas are eligible for Graduate Teaching Assistantships (TA). Teaching Assistants work up to ten (10) hours per week assisting faculty members with laboratory instruction, grading undergraduate quizzes, and conducting tutorial sessions. TA appointments carry a full tuition waiver plus a monthly living stipend of $600 during instructional months.

### Section 18.2 Doctoral Research Fellowship (DRF) Guidelines
Doctoral candidates enrolled in Ph.D. programs at NIT receive the Doctoral Research Fellowship (DRF) for up to four (4) years, renewable annually subject to satisfactory progress in research. DRF Fellows receive complete tuition exemption, a monthly stipend of $1,200, and an annual contingency research grant of $1,500 for conference travel and experimental materials. Renewal requires approval of the candidate's Doctoral Advisory Committee and publication of at least one peer-reviewed journal paper per year after candidacy confirmation.

## Section 19.0 Industry Sponsored Awards & Endowments

### Section 19.1 Alumni Endowment Memorial Scholarships
Established through donations from NIT alumni, Alumni Endowment Scholarships support high-achieving third-year and fourth-year undergraduate students demonstrating exceptional innovation in capstone design projects or open-source software contributions. Awards range from $1,000 to $3,000 as a one-time grant applied directly toward semester fee accounts.

### Section 19.2 Corporate Sponsored Academic Excellence Medals
Corporate partners of NIT sponsor annual gold and silver medals for graduating seniors achieving top academic rank in specialized domains such as Embedded Systems, Cloud Computing, Sustainable Energy, and Structural Engineering. Winners are recognized formally during the Annual University Convocation Ceremony.

## Section 20.0 Forfeiture, Appeals & Misrepresentation Penalty

### Section 20.1 Immediate Forfeiture of Financial Aid
Any student receiving institutional financial aid, scholarships, or assistantships who is found guilty of academic dishonesty, examination cheating, property destruction, or substance abuse by the Disciplinary Committee will suffer immediate and permanent forfeiture of all financial awards. The student will be required to repay all tuition fee waivers received during the active academic year within thirty (30) days.

### Section 20.2 Financial Aid Appeals Procedure
A student whose scholarship renewal is denied due to falling short of CGPA thresholds by less than 0.10 points may submit a formal appeal to the Scholarship Appeals Committee within seven (7) calendar days of notice. If the shortfall was caused by documented medical hospitalization, the committee may grant a one-semester probation window to restore the CGPA to the required threshold without revoking the financial award.
