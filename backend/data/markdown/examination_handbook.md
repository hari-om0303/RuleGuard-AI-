# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Examination & Evaluation Handbook (2025-2026 Edition)

## Document Information
- **Document Code**: NIT-EXAM-2025-v2
- **Issuing Authority**: Controller of Examinations & Academic Evaluation Committee
- **Scope**: Covers all rules, protocols, schedules, revaluations, and medical exemption guidelines for undergraduate and postgraduate examinations at Northbridge Institute of Technology.

## Section 7.0 End-Semester Examination Guidelines & Absences

### Section 7.1 Conduct of End-Semester Examinations
End-semester examinations (ESE) are conducted centrally by the Controller of Examinations at the conclusion of each regular semester. The examination schedule is published at least four (4) weeks prior to the commencement of the exam period. Examinations are conducted in written, practical, or digital formats depending on course specifications. Every student must present a valid physical Student Identification Card and an official Examination Admit Card to enter the examination hall.

### Section 7.2 Hall Ticket Eligibility and Clearance
Admit Cards (Hall Tickets) are generated electronically via the Student Portal seven (7) days prior to the start of examinations. An Admit Card will only be issued to students who satisfy three cumulative conditions: (a) minimum attendance requirement in all registered courses, (b) complete settlement of all tuition, hostel, and library fee dues, and (c) absence of active disciplinary bar issued by the Disciplinary Committee.

### Section 7.3 Unexcused Absence in Examinations
If a student fails to appear for a scheduled End-Semester Examination without prior written sanction or an approved medical exemption, the student will be marked as 'Absent' and awarded zero (0) marks for the examination component, resulting in an automatic grade of 'F' for the course.

### Section 7.4 Medical Absences and Attendance Condonation
Students with an approved medical exemption processed through the Student Health Center may appear for end-semester examinations with attendance as low as 65% in the affected course. To claim this medical condonation, the student or their authorized representative must submit a formal medical certificate issued by a registered medical officer along with hospital admission records to the Chief Medical Officer at the NIT Student Health Center within five (5) working days of returning to campus. Upon verification, the Health Center issues a Medical Exemption Certificate to the Controller of Examinations.

### Section 7.5 Provision for Scribes and Accommodation for Differently-Abled Students
Students with temporary physical injuries (such as bone fractures) or permanent physical disabilities affecting writing capability may apply to the Controller of Examinations for a approved Scribe and extra time allocation (20 minutes per hour of examination). Application for scribe allocation must be submitted at least seven (7) days prior to exam commencement with medical panel validation.

## Section 8.0 Fee Payment, Tuition Deadlines & Penalties

### Section 8.1 Late Fee Payment and Penalty Surcharge
Late fee payment is permitted within 10 calendar days after the published due date upon payment of a flat late penalty fee of $50. All tuition and fee payments must be remitted through the official NIT Student Financial Portal using approved electronic payment gateways, direct bank transfer, or certified bank draft. Cash payments are strictly prohibited at the Accounts Counter.

### Section 8.2 Registration Surcharge for Second-Week Payment
If a student attempts to complete semester registration during the second week of regular classes (Days 8 through 14 of the semester calendar), an additional Administrative Late Registration Surcharge of $100 will be levied in addition to any applicable late fee penalties. Registrations attempted after Day 14 of the semester will not be processed under any circumstances.

### Section 8.3 Installment Payment Plans and Hardship Applications
Under exceptional financial hardship, a student may apply to the Student Financial Aid Office for an Installment Payment Plan. If approved, the student is permitted to pay 50% of the total semester tuition fee on or before the main deadline, and the remaining 50% in two equal monthly installments. Installment applications must be submitted at least fifteen (15) days prior to the tuition fee deadline.

### Section 8.4 Scholarship Fee Deductions & Tuition Offset Guidelines
Students awarded Institute Merit Scholarships or State Financial Grants will have their tuition fee amounts automatically credited to their portal accounts prior to the payment deadline. Scholarship recipients are responsible for paying remaining fee components (such as hostel, dining, and laboratory fees) by the standard published deadline date.

## Section 9.0 Supplementary Examinations & Revaluation

### Section 9.1 Supplementary Examinations
Supplementary examinations are organized once annually during the Summer Term for students who earned a grade of 'F' or 'E' in any theory course during the preceding Fall or Spring semesters, provided the student had satisfied the attendance eligibility requirement during the regular semester. A student may register for a maximum of four (4) supplementary course examinations during a single Summer Term. The highest letter grade awarded in a supplementary examination is capped at 'C' (7.0 Grade Points).

### Section 9.2 Revaluation & Answer Script Inspection Protocol
A student who is dissatisfied with their awarded mark in an end-semester theory examination may apply for Answer Script Inspection and Revaluation. The application must be filed online through the Academic Portal within ten (10) working days from the official date of grade publication, accompanied by a non-refundable revaluation fee of $30 per course.

### Section 9.3 Revaluation Result Processing and Grade Revision
Upon receipt of a revaluation application, the Controller of Examinations assigns the anonymized answer script to an independent external examiner for re-evaluation. If the re-evaluated mark differs from the original mark by more than 15% of the maximum marks, a second external examiner evaluates the script, and the average of the two closest marks is awarded. If revaluation results in a grade change, the updated grade is reflected in the transcript within twenty (20) working days, and the revaluation fee is fully refunded.

## Section 10.0 Course Registration Adjustments & Special Drops

### Section 10.1 Emergency Course Drops for Medical Reasons
In cases of severe prolonged illness, major surgery, or serious physical trauma rendering a student incapable of continuing academic coursework for more than four (4) consecutive weeks, the student may petition the Academic Steering Committee for an Emergency Medical Course Drop. Such petitions must be accompanied by comprehensive inpatient medical reports and certification from a government hospital. If approved, all registered courses for that semester will be marked with grade 'W' without affecting academic standing.

### Section 10.2 Special Course Registration Adjustments
All requests for course withdrawal or drop must be submitted and approved before the end of Week 6 of the active semester; no withdrawals are permitted after Week 6 under any circumstances. Any request received after Week 6 will be automatically rejected by the Registrar's Office, and the student will remain enrolled in the course, subject to standard grading and evaluation criteria.
