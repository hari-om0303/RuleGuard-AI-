# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Student Code of Conduct & Residential Life (2025-2026 Edition)

## Document Information
- **Document Code**: NIT-LIFE-2025-v3
- **Issuing Authority**: Office of the Dean of Student Affairs & Student Disciplinary Committee
- **Scope**: Governs student behavior, academic integrity, residential housing, disciplinary procedures, financial enforcement, and grievance mechanisms at Northbridge Institute of Technology.

---

## Section 11.0 Student Disciplinary Code & Academic Misconduct

### Section 11.1 Definition of Academic Misconduct
Northbridge Institute of Technology maintains zero tolerance for academic dishonesty and ethical breaches. Academic misconduct includes, but is not limited to: (a) plagiarism in project reports, assignments, or published papers; (b) cheating or copying during mid-semester or end-semester examinations; (c) impersonation of another student during examinations or attendance checks; (d) unauthorized possession of examination papers or solution keys prior to exams; and (e) falsification of academic documents, medical certificates, or attendance records.

### Section 11.2 Sanctions for Unauthorized Notes in Examination Halls
What happens if a student is caught bringing unauthorized notes into an examination hall? Any student found in possession of unauthorized written notes, mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, or electronic communication devices inside an examination hall—regardless of whether the materials were actively used—will immediately have their examination paper confiscated by the invigilator. The Proctorial Board will issue a formal penalty including an automatic grade of 'F' in the course, suspension from all campus activities for one semester, and a permanent record of academic dishonesty on their transcript.

### Section 11.3 Plagiarism Penalties in Capstone Projects and Dissertations
All final year undergraduate capstone project reports, master's theses, and doctoral dissertations are subjected to mandatory automated plagiarism screening prior to defense. If Similarity Index exceeds 15% (excluding references and standard citations), the student must rewrite and resubmit the report within thirty (30) days. If Similarity Index exceeds 30%, the project is rejected, the student is awarded grade 'F', and must repeat the project across a full semester.

## Section 12.0 Administrative Deadlines & Account Registration

### Section 12.1 Student Record Maintenance and Address Updates
Every student is required to maintain accurate personal, guardian, and residential address details on the NIT Academic Information System. Any change of permanent address, phone number, or emergency contact information must be updated on the portal within seven (7) calendar days of the change. Official communications mailed to the address listed on the portal are legally deemed delivered.

### Section 12.2 Institutional Identity Cards and Campus Clearance
All students are issued a digital and physical NIT Identification Card upon admission. The ID card serves as an official credential for library borrowing, laboratory access, dining hall entry, and examination hall verification. Defacing, lending, or duplicating an ID card is a punishable breach of campus conduct regulations.

### Section 12.3 Account Suspension for Administrative Delinquency
Failure to return borrowed library books, failure to return athletic equipment, or non-payment of library fines within thirty (30) days of notification will result in an Administrative Hold placed on the student's portal account. An Administrative Hold prevents course registration, transcript generation, grade viewing, and hall ticket issuance.

### Section 12.4 Financial Non-Compliance and Account Suspension
Failure to complete full tuition and fee payment by the published semester deadline results in immediate cancellation of semester registration and expulsion from campus housing without any grace period. Students subjected to registration cancellation under Section 12.4 are deregistered from all classes, lose library borrowing rights, and must formally re-apply for readmission in the following academic year upon paying all outstanding balances plus a readmission processing fee of $300.

## Section 13.0 Hostel Accommodation & Residential Regulations

### Section 13.1 Hostel Occupancy Eligibility & Allocation
On-campus residential housing in NIT hostels is allocated on a semester basis to full-time enrolled students in good academic and financial standing. Priority allocation is given to first-year undergraduate students and international students. Hostel rooms are single or dual-occupancy and come furnished with standard study furniture.

### Section 13.2 Hostel Curfew and Visitor Protocols
All NIT residential halls observe a mandatory night curfew. All resident students must return to their assigned hostel premises by 10:30 PM on weekdays (Sunday through Thursday) and 11:30 PM on weekends (Friday and Saturday). Visitors and non-resident guests are permitted in hostel common lounges between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM only and are strictly barred from entering student residential corridors or individual rooms.

### Section 13.3 Prohibited Items and Substances in Residential Halls
The possession, consumption, storage, or distribution of alcoholic beverages, illegal narcotics, tobacco products, vaping devices, firearms, weapons, fireworks, open-flame heating appliances, or heavy power equipment inside hostel rooms or campus premises is strictly prohibited. Violation of substance policies results in immediate eviction from campus housing and referral to the Disciplinary Board for expulsion proceedings.

## Section 14.0 Student Grievances, Appeals Committee & Due Process

### Section 14.1 Merit Scholarships and Financial Assistance Framework
Northbridge Institute of Technology awards the Dean's Merit Scholarship to top-ranking undergraduate students in each department. To qualify for the Dean's Merit Scholarship, a student must: (a) maintain a CGPA of 9.00 or higher; (b) complete at least 18 credits per regular semester; (c) have no backlogs or 'F' grades in any course; and (d) have a clean disciplinary record with zero misconduct violations. The scholarship covers 50% of annual tuition fees.

### Section 14.2 Academic Grievance Redressal Committee Structure
Students who believe they have been subjected to improper academic evaluation, unfair treatment, or discrimination by any faculty member or department may file a formal petition with the Academic Grievance Redressal Committee (AGRC). The AGRC consists of the Dean of Student Affairs, two senior faculty professors, one student union representative, and an external legal ombudsman.

### Section 14.3 Grievance Hearing and Appeals Procedure
Upon receiving a written grievance petition through the Portal, the AGRC conducts a preliminary review within seven (7) working days. If the grievance is admitted for hearing, both the student petitioner and the respondent faculty member are invited to present evidence and oral statements. The AGRC issues its binding decision within twenty-one (21) calendar days of admission. Decisions of the AGRC may be appealed to the Academic Council within seven (7) days, whose ruling is final.
