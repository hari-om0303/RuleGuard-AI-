# Northbridge Institute of Technology — Hostel & Student Residence Manual (2025-2026 Edition)

## Document Information
- **Document Code**: NIT-HOSTEL-2025-v1
- **Issuing Authority**: Chief Warden & Office of Student Housing Services
- **Scope**: Outlines room allocation policies, mess dining rules, visitor entry protocols, quiet hours, internet access guidelines, and emergency evacuation procedures for all residential halls at Northbridge Institute of Technology.

---

## Section 21.0 Residence Hall Allocation & Facilities

### Section 21.1 Hostel Eligibility & Priority Criteria
On-campus residential housing at Northbridge Institute of Technology is provided to facilitate academic immersion and community living. Accommodation is available to full-time registered undergraduate and postgraduate students in good financial standing. First-year undergraduate students and international students are granted priority room allocation. Upper-year students are allocated rooms based on CGPA merit and clean disciplinary history.

### Section 21.2 Room Occupancy, Inventory & Furnishings
Standard student rooms are allocated on a single or double occupancy basis. Each resident is provided with a bed, study desk, ergonomic chair, bookshelf, wardrobe, and high-speed Ethernet port. Upon occupying the assigned room, residents must complete a Room Inventory Form within forty-eight (48) hours and report any pre-existing damage to the Hostel Caretaker. Residents are financially responsible for any damage or defacement to room fixtures occurring during their occupancy.

### Section 21.3 Campus Dining Hall & Mess Subscription Rules
Hostel room allocation includes mandatory subscription to the central NIT Campus Dining Hall. The mess fee covers three main meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) plus evening snacks seven days a week. Students requiring specialized medical diets must submit a prescription from the Student Health Center to the Chief Mess Supervisor. Mess fee rebates of 50% are granted for continuous approved absences exceeding seven (7) days during official academic vacations.

## Section 22.0 Residential Discipline & Safety Protocols

### Section 22.1 Night Curfew & Out-Pass Regulations
All undergraduate residential halls observe a strict night curfew. Resident students must return to their assigned hostel premises by 10:30 PM on weekdays (Sunday through Thursday) and 11:30 PM on weekends (Friday and Saturday). Any student intending to remain away from the hostel overnight must obtain an approved digital Out-Pass through the Hostel Student Portal at least twelve (12) hours prior to departure, signed off by their parent or registered guardian.

### Section 22.2 Visitor Regulations & Guest Accommodation
Visitors, family members, and non-resident guests are permitted inside hostel common reception lounges between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM only. Under no circumstances are visitors allowed to enter individual student residential corridors or student bedrooms. Guest rooms may be reserved for visiting parents for a maximum of three (3) consecutive nights upon paying the standard guest tariff of $25 per night, subject to availability.

### Section 22.3 Electrical Appliances & Energy Conservation Policy
To prevent electrical overloads and fire hazards, students are strictly prohibited from using high-wattage personal electrical appliances inside dorm rooms—including electric hot plates, induction cooktops, space heaters, microwave ovens, air conditioners, and immersion water heaters. Permissible appliances are limited to laptops, mobile phone chargers, table lamps, personal fans, and hair dryers under 1000W. Using unauthorized high-power appliances results in a flat fine of $100 and confiscation of the appliance.

### Section 22.4 Quiet Hours & Noise Pollution Guidelines
Hostels maintain mandatory Quiet Hours from 11:00 PM to 6:00 AM daily to ensure an atmosphere conducive to study and sleep. During Quiet Hours, playing loud musical instruments, operating high-volume sound systems, yelling in corridors, or holding noisy gatherings is strictly prohibited. Repeated noise violations will lead to disciplinary proceedings and potential eviction from campus housing.

## Section 23.0 Campus Network & IT Usage Guidelines

### Section 23.1 High-Speed Campus Wi-Fi & Bandwidth Limits
NIT provides encrypted high-speed Wi-Fi and Ethernet connectivity across all residential halls. Each student is allocated a monthly data quota of 150 GB on the high-speed tier, after which connection speed is throttled to 2 Mbps for the remainder of the calendar month. Wi-Fi access credentials are strictly personal and must not be shared.

### Section 23.2 Cybersecurity Policy & Prohibited Online Activities
Using the NIT network infrastructure for illegal file sharing, torrenting copyrighted commercial content, launching cyberattacks, hosting unauthorized web servers, accessing illicit websites, or sending spam/phishing emails is strictly illegal. The IT Infrastructure Cell monitors network traffic patterns automatically. Cyber safety breaches result in immediate network access revocation and referral to the Proctorial Board.

## Section 24.0 Emergency Evacuation & Health Safety

### Section 24.1 Fire Safety Drills & Evacuation Protocols
Fire evacuation drills are conducted unannounced twice per academic year in all hostel blocks. Residents must familiarize themselves with the posted evacuation route diagrams and emergency exit stairwells. Tampering with fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, or fire alarm pull stations is a serious safety violation incurring a $200 fine and disciplinary suspension.

### Section 24.2 Medical Emergencies & Ambulance Service
In the event of sudden medical illness or physical injury occurring inside hostel premises, residents must immediately notify the Resident Warden or Duty Security Guard. The NIT Emergency Health Vehicle operates 24/7 to transport sick students to the Student Health Center or local referral hospital free of charge.

## Section 25.0 Summer Storage, Vacation Rules & Room Vacating

### Section 25.1 Summer Break Room Clearance Procedure
At the conclusion of the Spring Semester examination period, all resident students who are not registered for Summer Term courses or approved summer research assistantships must completely vacate their rooms within forty-eight (48) hours of their final examination. All personal belongings must be removed, furniture returned to its original arrangement, and room keys surrendered to the Hostel Administrative Office. Rooms left occupied or unvacated past the deadline will be cleared by campus security at the owner's expense.

### Section 25.2 Centralized Summer Luggage Storage Facility
Students returning to campus housing for the subsequent Fall Semester may store a maximum of two (2) sealed, labeled luggage boxes in the Centralized Hostel Luggage Store Room during the summer recess. Storage is provided at a nominal administrative fee of $10 per box. The Institute accepts no financial liability for fragile, perishable, or high-value electronic items stored in the communal store room.

### Section 25.3 Residential Council & Floor Warden Election Rules
Each residential hostel block elects two floor wardens per floor and one Chief Student Residential Representative annually during the second week of the Fall Semester. Candidates must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.00 and have zero disciplinary warnings. Floor wardens coordinate residence welfare meetings, monitor common room equipment, and liaise directly with the Hostel Management Committee.
